Calen Young

Business Administration Student | UC Riverside

Hi, I'm Calen Young, a Business Administration student at the University of California, Riverside, expected to graduate in June 2028. I'm from Irvine, California, and before starting at UCR I completed coursework at Irvine Valley College in microeconomics, macroeconomics, and calculus. Those classes sparked my interest in how markets, companies, and financial decisions fit together, and that interest drives my goal of building a career in finance. I am currently seeking a Summer 2027 internship where I can apply what I am learning in the classroom to real business challenges.

Leadership has been a constant thread through everything I do. In Winter 2026, I served as President of the Highlander Business Society's Career Development Program class at UCR. In that role I kept our cohort on track with deadlines and assignments, ran meetings, delegated tasks, and oversaw our presentations, fundraisers, and professional and community events from planning through execution. The experience taught me how to hold a team accountable while keeping everyone motivated, and it pushed me to grow as a public speaker and project manager.

Before college, I spent three years as a varsity starter on the Woodbridge High School golf team and was elected Co-Captain by my teammates and coach for two of those years. I led practices, helped prepare the team for matches, and mentored underclassmen on discipline, sportsmanship, and a competitive mindset. Our team qualified for the CIF Championships in 2021 and 2022. Golf also gave me my first job: as an instructor at Slobo Golf Academy in Newport Beach, I coached more than 15 beginner golfers on swing mechanics, putting, and course etiquette, and learned how to give clear, personalized feedback that helps people improve.

I also believe in using my skills to help my community. During the COVID-19 pandemic, I co-founded Irvine Ask For Mask, a small nonprofit that raised over $1,500 through GoFundMe to buy personal protective equipment. We distributed masks to elderly residents in Irvine and to healthcare workers in Honolulu, Hawaii, and I handled much of the outreach, logistics, and donor communication. More recently, I worked as a host at California Pizza Kitchen in Irvine, where I managed seating, waitlists, and guest flow during busy dinner rushes. That job sharpened my customer service, communication, and ability to stay organized under pressure.

Outside of work and school, I enjoy following the stock market and learning about investing, playing golf, and spending time outdoors. I'm always looking for opportunities to learn, take on responsibility, and connect with people who share my interests. Feel free to reach out through LinkedIn or email using the links below.
